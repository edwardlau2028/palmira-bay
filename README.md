PALMIRA BAY — Streets of Sun & Gold: an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Play Rico Vega, a getaway driver working for the fixer Mari Duarte, across a 480×480 golden-dusk coastal city: downtown glass towers, the old town, the harbour, the beach and lighthouse. Walk or drive six original cars (plus police cruisers), steal cars, dodge a 0–5 star wanted level with pursuing police cars, foot police and roadblocks, run 7 story missions plus a finale heist, fight with fists and a pistol, hunt 8 hidden gold bars, and save your progress. HUD with a live minimap, wanted stars, cash, health/armour, clock and three radio stations; all art, sound and music are original and synthesized in the browser. Runs offline from static files, no network request.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Controls: WASD move/drive, Shift sprint, mouse drag to look, E steal/enter/exit car, F punch, click shoot, Space handbrake, R/1-3 radio, C camera distance, M mute, P pause, S save.
